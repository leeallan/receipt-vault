# Privacy Policy — Receipt Vault

**Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

Receipt Vault ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is provided by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY — e.g. Far Above Studios] ("the developer"). This policy explains what information the app handles, how, and your rights over it. We've tried to keep it plain.

## The short version

- Your receipts — the photos and the details — are stored **entirely on your device**. Text recognition happens **on your device**; your receipt images are not sent anywhere to be read.
- We operate **no servers** and there is **no account**. We do **not** collect your name, email, payment details, or receipt content. Nothing is uploaded to us.
- Purchases are handled by **Apple**. Your Premium unlock is recorded by Apple against your Apple Account; we never receive or store your transaction or payment details.
- There is **no cloud backup and no online sync**. To keep a copy of your data elsewhere, use the app's Export feature, which creates a file you choose where to send.

## Information the app handles

**Receipt content (on your device).** Photos you capture, and the details recognised or entered (merchant, total, date, category, line items, notes, tags), are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

**Purchase information.** When you buy the one-time Premium unlock, Apple processes the payment and records your purchase against your Apple Account. The app confirms your entitlement directly with the App Store on your device — we run no server and never receive or store your transaction identifier, name, email, or payment details. If you reinstall or change device, "Restore Purchases" re-checks your entitlement with Apple.

**Diagnostic information.** The app does not include third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs. Apple may provide us with aggregate, anonymous crash and usage statistics if you have opted in to sharing these with developers in your device settings.

## On-device processing

Text recognition (reading the merchant, total, and line items from a receipt) is performed **on your device** using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your receipt images are not uploaded for processing.

## No cloud, no account

Receipt Vault does **not** require an account and does **not** upload your receipts anywhere. There is no online sync and no cloud backup — your data lives only on your device. To keep a copy elsewhere, use the app's **Export** (CSV, or PDF with Premium), which creates a file you can save or share wherever you choose. Any exported file is then handled by whatever service you send it to, under that service's terms.

## How we use information

Information is used only on your device — to recognise and organise your receipts and to generate the exports and reports you ask for. Unlocking Premium is handled by Apple. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising, and because nothing reaches us there is no data for us to share.

## Third parties

- **Apple** — processes payments and provides the App Store. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

That is the only third party involved in running Receipt Vault. We operate no servers of our own and use no analytics providers, advertising networks, or data brokers.

## Payments

All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

## Retention and deletion

- **On-device data** stays until you delete individual receipts or delete the app; deleting the app removes it. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf.
- **Your Premium purchase** is recorded by Apple against your Apple Account under Apple's terms.

## Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by iOS and your device passcode/biometrics. Because we operate no servers, there is no online store of your data to be breached. Files you export are only as private as the place you choose to send them. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping everything local removes most of the risk.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under the UK GDPR / GDPR, including to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because all of your data is on your device, you can exercise these directly in the app: view, edit, export, or delete your receipts at any time. For anything relating to your purchase, contact Apple. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

We operate no servers and transfer no data internationally. Your data stays on your device wherever you are. Payment processing is carried out by Apple under Apple's own infrastructure and terms.
